04Run it

# Launch python -m sglang.launch_server --model-path zai-org/GLM-5.2 --tp 8 --quantization fp8 --enable-moe-ep --kv-cache-dtype fp8_e5m2 --context-length 131072 --port 8000
  • 8 × 300 W plus two 280 W CPUs and the platform: about 3.2 kW, a dedicated 240 V circuit and a room that can shed the heat.
  • Same shape as the cloud node, which is the point: the launch line is identical, only the owner changes. Per-stream speed follows memory bandwidth, GDDR7 at 1.8 TB/s per card against the B200's 8 TB/s, so the cloud node decodes faster; this box is FP8 parity you own.
  • Six cards (576 GB) cannot hold the FP8 checkpoint at all, and eight leave about 24 GB for KV. For FP8 at full concurrency and the 1M window, the cloud node's 1,536 GB is the shape.
