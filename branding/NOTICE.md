# Branding asset attributions

## Claw mark
The claw in the FlatClaw mark and wordmarks (`mark.svg`, `wordmark*.svg`, the Kirk | FlatClaw lockups, and the site and Portal app icons) is "Claw Slashes" by **Lorc**, sourced from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/1x1/lorc/claw-slashes.html), licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The original artwork is unmodified beyond color: FlatClaw navy `#22314A` on light surfaces, white `#FFFFFF` on navy and dark grounds, and FlatClaw blue `#0099FF` for the app icons. No claim of ownership over the underlying mark is made.

## Wordmark
"flatclaw" and the PRIVATE AI PLATFORM subline are set in **Inter** (Rasmus Andersson, [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org)) and outlined, so the files carry no font dependency.
